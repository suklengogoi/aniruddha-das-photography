Your wedding, told the way it felt.

Aniruddha Das Photography is a team of wedding photographers based in Beltola, Guwahati. Our style is contemporary and artistic, and we care about the small things: the glance before the vows, the gold on a grandmother’s hand, the light in the room.

We cover everything from the pre-wedding shoot to the last ritual, then edit and design your album ourselves, so the wedding book you keep is unmistakably yours. And we are always happy to travel to wherever you are celebrating.
