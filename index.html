About the studio

We stay close enough to catch the feeling, and quiet enough that you forget we are there.

Aniruddha Das Photography is a team of wedding photographers based in Beltola, Guwahati. Our style is contemporary and artistic, and we care about the small things: the glance before the vows, the gold on a grandmother’s hand, the light in the room.

We cover everything from the pre-wedding shoot to the last ritual, then edit and design your album ourselves. And we are always happy to travel to wherever you are celebrating.
